## Editorial boundaries

- This batch has distinct search intent from the first seven articles. Existing application, form,
  exam-day and OBP pages remain the hubs. Related-article targets are planning metadata, not links
  to unpublished routes in the article bodies.
- All bodies preserve an explicit 2026 source-scope note. They do not assert that the same conditions
  apply to 2027 or any later examination.
- Calendar deadlines and obsolete application windows are omitted. The open-high-school registration
  boundary and legacy undergraduate extra-score boundary are eligibility criteria, reproduced exactly
  as dated source conditions, not generated application deadlines.
- The open-high-school article does not determine an individual applicant's status or graduation
  eligibility. Term counts are evaluated at application time; exam eligibility and university
  enrolment requirements are kept separate.
- The education-data article explains the prescribed confirmation step and mentions the documented
  fallback without suggesting that confirmation can be skipped. No loss-of-rights claim was invented.
- The answer-sheet article keeps procedural rules separate from optional mock-exam habits. It does
  not reproduce exam questions or answer keys, and does not include criminal-law advice.
- The vocational-school article explains how to find the relevant official table. No unverified
  school-field/program pairing or current programme code was invented. Its 80-grade example assumes
  documented eligibility and no coefficient reduction: OBP 400, standard contribution 48, additional
  contribution 24, combined contributions 72. That total is not a complete examination score.
- A later programme/preference article needs the relevant year's programmes-and-quotas guide and
  amendments. Additional placement and special-aptitude procedures need their own official guides.
- At drafting, author, verification and publication metadata were null. The initial writing pass changed no database,
  image storage, seed or application runtime. Four licensed Pexels covers were subsequently added on
  2026-11-03 in the existing development media bucket, with cover fields/local assets and body credits.
  See `media-sources.json` and `images/README.md`; the image-preparation step did not publish records.
  The four articles were subsequently published locally on 2026-11-03 with organisation author and
  explicitly automated source verification. `publication-local.json` and
  `publication-verification-local.json` record the actual operation and public-read checks.
