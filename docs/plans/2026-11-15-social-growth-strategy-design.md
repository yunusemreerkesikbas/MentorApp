## Intent

PuhuKoç is in waitlist mode because payment will be taken only on mobile (web payment through
iyzico is blocked by a tax question). The mobile app is expected in about 30 days (around
mid-December 2026). Until then the job of social media is awareness and a first waitlist; at
launch it is installs; after launch it is steady organic growth with a small budget.

## Owner decisions (2026-11-15)

- Launch = the mobile app going live, about 30 days out.
- No paid ads before launch.
- First-stage success: 100 waitlist signups.
- The owner does not appear on camera. Creators or agencies may.
- Budget ceiling: 10.000–15.000 TL per month, tools included.
- Students first; coach marketing later.
- High-school students are in scope. LGS can wait.

## Goals

| Phase | Window | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Before launch | now → launch day | 100 waitlist signups (owner). 1.000 followers across Instagram and TikTok, one video above 10.000 views *(my call)*. |
| 2 · Launch week | launch day + 7 | Waitlist and followers converted to installs. Numeric target set once store dates are known. |
| 3 · After launch | 60 days | A repeatable weekly routine, the first paid test, a measured cost per signup. |

## Non-goals

- Paid ads in Phase 1. Agency retainers (do not fit the budget, *my call*, not price-checked).
- Coach acquisition, LGS and parent messaging (after Phase 3).
- X and LinkedIn as growth channels (the two kit cards stay as profile dressing).
- Auto-publishing without the owner's approval. Auto-replies to comments or DMs.

## Anti-goals

- Everything in the launch kit's anti-goal list (FOMO copy, mocking failure, explaining
  invite-only or KVKK in posts, "AI" in Turkish copy, calm Puhu).
- Fake engagement: bought followers, paid comment flooding, undisclosed sponsorships.
- Feature claims the app does not keep at the time of posting.
- Many look-alike brand accounts run from one device (platform-ban risk).

## Constraints

- Under-18 users cannot be targeted by interest on Meta or TikTok. High-school reach is
  organic and through 18+ creators whose followers are in high school.
- Signup is invite-only until launch. A creator's invite link is the only way in.
- Every post is the owner's approval, one by one. Accounts, payments and store panels are the
  owner's hands only.
- Creator posts carry the platform's paid-partnership label or `#işbirliği`, also when the
  payment is early access.
- Audience this season is YKS-heavy (2026 YKS: 2.425.560 applicants). KPSS Lisans appears to be
  biennial, next in 2028; **verify with ÖSYM** before any KPSS-specific campaign.

## Approaches

| | A · Student ambassadors | B · Puhu character account | C · Paid ads |
|---|---|---|---|
| What | 5–8 small accounts get early access and film a real week with Puhu | Puhu-only short videos and save-worthy study content | Meta and TikTok ads to the waitlist |
| High-school reach | Yes | Yes, slowly | Weak |
| Lasting value | Real user videos, reusable as ads later | Account and archive grow; easiest to automate | Stops with the budget |
| Risk | Finding and managing creators takes effort | Slow reach, faceless trust | No revenue to pay it back |

**Chosen: A as the spine, B as the base. C is out for Phase 1** (owner: no paid ads now) and
returns in Phase 3 as a small test. In Phase 1 ambassadors are paid in early access, not money
*(my call, from "no need to spend in this period")*.

## Positioning

"Yalnız çalışmak yasak." PuhuKoç is not lesson content. It is the layer on top of whatever the
student already uses: a plan, a focus session, a table with other people, a weekly recap. Lesson
platforms are complements, not rivals. Human coaching costs thousands of TL a month; Puhu is the
everyday version.

## Content lanes

| Lane | What | Source | Share of the week |
|---|---|---|---|
| Puhu oynuyor | Puhu as a character in 6–12 s native videos: POV jokes, trend sounds, "Puhu iş başında" jobs | New clips (owner generates from prompts), kit reels | 3 videos |
| İşe yarar | Saveable carousels: weekly plan template, how to review a deneme, 25-minute session guide | Kit style C (krem defter), new copy | 1 carousel |
| Vitrin | Feature and brand posts | Existing kit inventory | 1–2 posts |
| Sen de varsın | Ambassador videos, comment replies turned into posts, polls | Ambassadors, community | Stories daily, reposts as they come |

Every video goes to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts (the owner opens the channel).
Captions end with one action: comment, save, or the bio link.

## Phase 1 · "Yerini kap" (now → launch)

- Week 1: publish F3 (waitlist post) and pin it. Bio link goes to `puhukoc.com/kayit` with UTM
  per platform. Start the weekly routine. Shortlist 15 ambassador candidates.
- Week 2: first ambassadors get invite links. Waitlist series: three stories and one reel built
  on "Masada yerin hazır". First "İşe yarar" carousel.
- Week 3: ambassador videos go out. Store listings submitted (see open items).
- Week 4: countdown posts once the date is firm. Premium pieces enter the calendar with the
  sale flag still off.

## Phase 2 · Launch week

- One message everywhere: the app is out. T1 film re-cut as the launch reel, T1P as the feed post.
- Waitlist email on launch morning (the transactional renderer exists; copy is new work).
- Ambassadors post on the same two days. Premium pieces switch to "7 GÜN ÜCRETSİZ DENE" only if
  in-app purchase is live that day.
- Bio link changes from the waitlist to the store links.

## Phase 3 · After launch (60 days)

- Semester-break "kamp" campaign (late January, date to confirm): a daily masa hour, weekly
  nickname cards shared by students.
- Share loops already in the app (weekly nickname card, session polaroid, `PUHU-` invite codes)
  become the main call to action.
- Budget starts: best two or three ambassadors paid for a second video; one small ad test to
  18+ graduates using the best-performing organic video.
- Per-exam series picked from the "Hangi sınava hazırlanıyorsun?" comments.

## Ambassador programme

Two rings (owner, 2026-11-15: start with people the owner knows):

- **Ring 1 · "İlk masa"**: the owner's friends and acquaintances who are preparing for an exam
  or recently did. They are the first real users: feedback, first comments and shares, honest
  quotes. They also fill a Google Play closed test if the store account needs one. No follower
  count required. Starts now.
- **Ring 2 · Ambassadors**: people the owner does not know, with their own study audience.
  They bring reach. Starts in week 2–3.

Premium for both rings: the admin panel has no "grant Premium" action today (entitlement comes
from a subscription or a coach seat; promotions are discounts). Before launch there is nothing
to gift, so the reward is early access through the existing invite link. At launch, use the
stores' own subscription promo codes *(my call; check they fit the in-app purchase setup)*. An
admin "grant Premium for N days" action is a separate small spec, only if store codes fall short.

Ring 2 details:

- Who: 18+, 2.000–30.000 followers, study or exam content, real engagement. Graduates and
  first-year university students with high-school followers.
- Offer in Phase 1: early access, a named place in the launch, a Premium code at launch.
- Ask: use the app for a week, one honest video, labelled as a partnership. No script; a short
  brief with three things worth showing and the claims to avoid.
- Process: Claude drafts the shortlist criteria, outreach message and brief. The owner sends the
  messages and issues invite links.

## Automation

| Step | Who | How |
|---|---|---|
| Monday report | Claude, scheduled | Metricool numbers per post, top and bottom three, what to repeat |
| Weekly drafts | Claude | Posts placed in Metricool as drafts with caption, alt text and time from best-time data |
| Approval | Owner | Approves or edits each draft; nothing publishes otherwise |
| Trend check | Claude, twice a week | Trending sounds and formats, three ideas that fit Puhu |
| Waitlist count | Owner | Read from the admin waitlist page, shared on Monday |
| Comments, DMs | Owner | Manual |

Supermetrics is dropped when the trial ends *(my call)*; Metricool plus GA4 cover reporting.

## Measurement

- North star for Phase 1: waitlist signups (`generate_lead`), split by UTM source.
- Weekly: reach, views, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks, follower change.
- Rule: a format that beats the account median twice gets repeated; one that loses three times
  is dropped.
